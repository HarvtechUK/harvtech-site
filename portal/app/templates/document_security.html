Security and data handling

{% include "_flash.html" %}

In one paragraph

The health check reads the configuration of your Azure estate with read-only roles you grant, prices it against Microsoft's public price list, and produces findings. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in your tenant. It reads no data planes: no blobs, files, table rows, secrets or database contents. What we hold about you is configuration metadata, metric summaries, directory metadata where you grant it, aggregated log query results where you grant them, and the findings themselves. It is stored in HarvTech's own Azure tenant in the UK, reachable only by HarvTech staff after sign-in, and deleted when the engagement ends or when you ask.

Every collector is read-only by construction. The scan identity holds no write permission, the scripts contain only listing and SELECT calls, and the engine has no code path that writes to a client tenant. Nothing is installed in your tenant.

Where it is held and who can see it

A PostgreSQL database in HarvTech's Azure subscription in UK South, encrypted in transit and at rest, with Entra authentication only. HarvTech staff reach it after signing in with a HarvTech account. No client data goes to any third party. The engine is deterministic rules: no AI model processes your data, and no telemetry leaves the environment. Microsoft Azure is the only sub-processor.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for the engagement and 90 days after the final report, so questions on the report can be answered, then deleted. You can ask for deletion at any time from the Requests page: every scan, snapshot and finding is removed in one operation and we confirm in writing.

The scan identity

The identity you create holds Reader and the optional read-only roles in the onboarding guide, and nothing else. HarvTech keeps its credential in Azure Key Vault, rotates it at the end of the engagement, and asks you to delete the identity when the engagement ends. If you prefer Azure Lighthouse, the delegation is Reader-only and you revoke it yourself.

This portal

Your report is published here as a snapshot: HarvTech reviews the findings and then places the report on your page. The portal holds that report and nothing else from the scan; the findings database itself is not reachable from here. Your named users sign in with their own Microsoft work accounts and see only your organisation's pages. The portal records when your users sign in, which pages they open and when the report is downloaded, so that we know it has reached you. It does not record what is typed or which finding was read.

What you can ask us to do

Delete your data, send you your findings, tell you who can see your data, rotate or retire the scan identity, pause scanning, or answer a question in writing. Each is a request on the Requests page, answered there, with a record of when.
