Azure health check

Onboarding guide

The health check reads your Azure estate and never changes it. Everything it needs is a read-only role, or a read-only script run by your own staff. Access is granted in tiers. Reader alone is a complete engagement; each further tier switches on more of the {{ total_rules }} checks, and the report says "not assessed" for whatever was not granted rather than staying silent.

You mark each step done on the onboarding page. HarvTech then verifies it with a read-only probe before the first scan, so nobody finds out on scan day that a role was assigned to the wrong identity.
